Shower & Supplies

Help run a neighborhood drop-in center. Give guests a warm shower, clean laundry, and the supplies they need.

How to play

Manage showers

Send guests to an open stall. Clean each stall after use.

Do the laundry

Start a wash, then move the guest’s laundry to the dryer.

Sort and give supplies

Match donations to their bins, then fill guest requests from stock.

Call volunteers

Spend 50 points to sort donations, clean stations, and ease stress.

Inspired by Hope’s Corner’s Shower & Laundry Program.
